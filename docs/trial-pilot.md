# Temporary allowance-only pilot

The owner explicitly authorized use of the existing Google free allowance and rejected any purchase or paid continuation. This supersedes their earlier sustainable-only answer. **TEMPORARY FREE — NOT SUSTAINABLE.** Oracle remains excluded and the expired anonymous Railway trial will not be renewed.

[Google's current trial terms](https://docs.cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features) state that an unupgraded trial is non-billable, ends at credit exhaustion or its deadline, and stops its resources. Subsequent deletion is possible. The agent never upgrades billing, purchases, enters payment information, renews a trial or accepts new legal terms. A signed/encrypted backup in the same trial account does not survive account loss reliably.

`gcp-free.py --cost-policy temporary-trial` requires fresh private observed trial evidence, the explicit allowance-only policy, an unupgraded non-billable account, at least $50 remaining credit, a cutoff at least seven days before expiry, current official terms and billing-lag review. It inventories every linked project and applies the same conservative resource topology checks: one eligible-size VM, local standard persistent disk, private regional encrypted-object bucket, IPv6 only, no public IPv4/NAT/load balancer. It does not claim a delayed zero-dollar report proves aggregate unused allowances. Trial credit coverage is distinct from sustainable Free Tier eligibility; `free_gate` retains its original non-trial requirements.

Host activation uses `bootstrap.py --trial-evidence <private-file>`. Before allowing writers, the trial guard requires a real signed isolated restore of a client-encrypted copy held outside the trial account, with a content hash and private destination evidence. Empty/synthetic commissioning fixtures cannot be recorded as operational recovery. Actual host commissioning can use prepare mode with non-billable credit evidence, which starts no containers, then prepare the images and create a signed bundle using isolated maintenance, transfer encrypted content to independent private custody, restore it there and record its actual checks. Only then can deploy mode start the persistent app. Evidence refresh uses atomic private replacement and refuses account or shutdown-deadline changes.

The trial guard runs before systemd startup and before every bounded health-recovery attempt. Trial containers use `restart: 'no'`, so Docker cannot resurrect writers before that check after a reboot. The existing minute health timer supplies bounded crash recovery while the guard permits it. Credit/account and independent recovery evidence must be refreshed within 12 hours; missing/stale evidence, the early cutoff, credit below reserve, paid conversion or failed restore causes a persistent STOP_WRITES latch and stops all containers. Existing corruption latches and incident evidence are preserved. No automatic latch clearing, database replacement, cloud deletion or billing upgrade occurs. Failed shutdown is reported, never asserted as successful.

This freshness requirement trades availability for recovery safety. Before pilot acceptance, actual independent backup transport and review refresh must run without relying on an always-on owner laptop for application serving. A private owner-held encrypted recovery copy may be independent archival custody; it is not evidence of continuously commissioned transport or an operational RPO. No secrets or account evidence belong in public GitHub.

The application stays pinned to approved commit `c05aba604a9827a3f5cc55f5fc8ba478e9ae54dc` and the images in `infra/release.json`. Geography, quarantined claims, governance, methodology or descriptive-only scope, Burmese field review, integration authorizations and field acceptance remain accountable human gates.
