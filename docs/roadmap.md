# Release and commissioning roadmap

P0/P1: immutable intake, authorization/session hardening, versioned review/corrections, provider checkpoint/outbox/retry, private contributor feedback and safe exports are implemented and tested. P2: field clocks, explainable intervals, sensitivity ordering, requirements/pledge/dispatch/delivery/receipt, ETA/expiry warnings, investigation worklists, interactive supplied-coordinate map and role-specific dashboard are implemented. P3: pinned production container, persistent host topology, safe probes, signed backups/restore, structured health and GitHub validation/image workflows are configured and locally tested.

Before launch, commission the host/domain/HTTPS, named accounts, bot/service keys and mapping, encrypted off-site recovery, monitoring and field acceptance. Review actual data/coordinates; approve models and reporting/retention policies. See [readiness](deployment-readiness.md) for component evidence and blockers.

V1: complete settlement gazetteer and boundaries/exposure layers; stronger source-independence and corroboration tooling; fully reviewed technical Burmese prose; operational time-to-review/delivery trends; managed identity/MFA; PostgreSQL/PostGIS before multi-host/high-availability operation. V2: statistically defensible uncertainty, calibrated probabilistic inference, Monte Carlo with validated distributions, SMS with approved cost/access and spatial silent-zone modeling. These remain unavailable when their assumptions or source inputs are absent. No missingness is converted into safety or fabricated emergency severity.
